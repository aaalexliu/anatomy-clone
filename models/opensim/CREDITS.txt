arm26.osim is the OpenSim Arm26 model.

Holzbaur, K.R.S., Murray, W.M., Delp, S.L. A Model of the Upper Extremity for
Simulating Musculoskeletal Surgery and Analyzing Neuromuscular Control.
Annals of Biomedical Engineering, vol 33, pp 829–840, 2005.

Adapted by the OpenSim Development Team (Reinbolt, J; Seth, A; Habib, A; Hamner, S)
from a model originally created by Katherine Holzbaur (11/22/04).

Geometry meshes are not included. Moment arms do not need them.

Rajagopal2016.osim and Geometry/ are the full-body gait model and its bone
meshes.

Rajagopal, A., Dembia, C.L., DeMers, M.S., Delp, D.D., Hicks, J.L., Delp, S.L.
Full-Body Musculoskeletal Model for Muscle-Driven Simulation of Normal and
Pathological Gait. IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, 2016.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
https://simtk.org/projects/full_body

The arms in that model are torque actuators. MOBL_ARMS_41.osim supplies the
shoulder muscle paths used for moment arms and the muscle tubes. It is the
right-arm MoBL-ARMS model (BSD-3-Clause; non-commercial terms are in the file
header).

Saul, K.R., Hu, X., Goehler, C.M., Vidt, M.E., Daly, M., Velisar, A., Murray, W.M.

an upper limb musculoskeletal model. Computer Methods in Biomechanics and
Biomedical Engineering, 2015.
